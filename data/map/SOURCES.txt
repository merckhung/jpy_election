data/map/japan.topo.json — sources and attribution
====================================================

japan.topo.json is generated by tools/build_data.py from:

1. Municipality boundaries: 国土交通省 国土数値情報「行政区域データ」(N03, 2021-01-01),
   as simplified TopoJSON by SmartNews Media Research Institute,
   https://github.com/smartnews-smri/japan-topography
   (出典：国土交通省国土数値情報ダウンロードサイト, https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/ —
   used under the 国土数値情報 terms of use, which permit reuse with attribution).
   Hamamatsu's former wards were merged into the 2024 wards (中央区/浜名区/天竜区).

2. Single-member district boundaries (2022 apportionment, 289 districts):
   西澤明（地域・交通データ研究所）「衆議院小選挙区 2022 区割りポリゴン」,
   https://gtfs-gis.jp/senkyoku2022/ — used only to split municipalities that
   straddle district lines. Please credit the author when redistributing.

3. District / counting-unit assignment: 総務省 (MIC) official municipality-level
   results of the 51st general election, https://www.soumu.go.jp/senkyo/senkyo_s/data/shugiin51/

Objects: prefectures (47), districts (289), units (1931 counting units:
municipalities / wards, parts of split municipalities have codes like 13111_03).
